# 🎵 SQL Music Store Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes a **Music Store database using SQL** to extract meaningful business insights related to customers, sales, artists, genres, invoices, and purchasing behavior.

The analysis is divided into **Easy, Moderate, and Advanced** SQL questions, covering different levels of data analysis and SQL concepts.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Identify the store's top customers and highest-spending cities
* Analyze invoice and sales trends across countries
* Find popular music genres and artists
* Identify top rock music listeners and artists
* Analyze customer spending by artist and country
* Find the most popular genre in each country
* Identify the highest-spending customer in each country

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### Easy Level

* Found the senior-most employee based on job level
* Identified countries with the highest number of invoices
* Found the top invoice values
* Identified the city generating the highest revenue
* Found the best customer based on total spending

### Moderate Level

* Identified customers who listen to Rock music
* Found the top 10 artists with the most Rock tracks
* Identified tracks longer than the average song length

### Advanced Level

* Analyzed customer spending on the best-selling artist
* Found the most popular music genre for each country
* Identified the highest-spending customer in each country
* Handled cases where multiple customers or genres share the top position
